# Low health warning assets

AirHorn.ogg: Air Horn by Mike Koenig, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), from SoundBible
(https://soundbible.com/), copied unchanged from the installed WeakAuras
Media/Sounds/AirHorn.ogg. Attribution verified against WeakAuras/Media/Provided by.txt.

AirHorn10.wav through AirHorn100.wav are volume-adjusted PCM conversions of
the same recording, retaining the above attribution and CC BY 3.0 license.
Reproduce them with scripts/generate_health_volumes.py and ffmpeg.

The warning logic is implemented directly in HardcoreBuddy. WeakAuras is not
required at runtime. The user's saved aura supplied the 40% threshold, red
72px text, half-second alpha pulse and play-on-start air horn settings.
